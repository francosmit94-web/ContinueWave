Why Practice Growth Breaks Down

Most practices do not need more scattered marketing activity. They need a clearer diagnosis of why new patient demand is weak, where inquiries are leaking, and why bookings do not convert reliably.

That is why the right first step is usually the audit, not a broad call. It gives you a cleaner read on what is actually broken before you decide on a larger engagement.
