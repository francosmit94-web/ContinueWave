Inclusion Criteria
- Include prior material only if it improves clarity, execution, decision quality, reusable assets, or operating structure.
- Include only what is still relevant to current work.
- Prefer material that can be reused cleanly without dragging old context with it.

Exclusion Criteria
- Exclude desperation, duplication, stale plans, vague philosophy, tool-shaped clutter, and anything that increases cognitive load.
- Exclude material that depends on old workflows, old naming, or abandoned offers.
- Exclude anything that is interesting but not useful now.

Evaluation Method
- Judge old material by usefulness, clarity, relevance, reusability, and structural cleanliness.
- If a document cannot justify its place quickly, leave it out.
- Rewrite useful ideas into ContinueWave language instead of importing old structure blindly.

First Synthesis Focus
- Active offers and their current positioning.
- AtlasFlow operating structure and service logic.
- Core operating rules that reduce friction and improve execution.
- High-value strategic documents that still help present decisions.
