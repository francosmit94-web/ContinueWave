# AtlasFlow First 5 Clients

## Goal
- Get the first 5 AtlasFlow clients by solving contact-flow friction fast.

## Best First Niches
- dentists
- physios
- aesthetic clinics
- guesthouses
- tutors

Current best default:
- medical and wellness

## Fast Lead Filter
In 10 seconds, reject or flag based on:
- no WhatsApp button
- buried contact page
- weak mobile contact flow
- no clear CTA

If one of those is obvious, it is a lead.

## Outreach Volume
- 20-30 messages per day
- aim for 5-10 replies
- aim for 1-2 calls
- expect 1 close every 2-3 days once the loop is moving

## Offer
- "I can make it easier for customers to contact you within 24-48 hours."

## Delivery Stack
- AtlasFlow page structure
- shared runtime tracking
- Vercel / Netlify deployment
- GA4 contact-intent tracking

## Contact Intent Definition
Track these as conversion candidates:
- `conversion_email_intent`
- `conversion_phone_intent`
- `conversion_whatsapp_intent`

Do not count:
- `form_submit_attempt`

## Daily Loop
1. Find business
2. Spot friction
3. Send message
4. Show quick fix
5. Implement
6. Track results
7. Repeat

## Rule
- Do not sell architecture.
- Do not sell AI.
- Do not sell systems.
- Sell easier contact and more inbound conversations.
